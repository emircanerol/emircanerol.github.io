Publications

CorefInst: Leveraging LLMs for Multilingual Coreference Resolution

Tuğba Pamay Arslan, Emircan Erol, Gülşen Eryiğit

Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics (TACL), vol. 14, 2026

paper arXiv

The first multilingual coreference resolution method built on decoder-only LLMs, handling both overt and zero mentions. We compare five instruction sets for casting coreference as an instruction-following task, with a controlled inference procedure, across Llama 3.1, Gemma 2 and Mistral 0.3. Our best model, a fully fine-tuned Llama 3.1, outperforms the previous state of the art (CorPipe 24) by 2 points on average across the languages of CorefUD v1.2.
